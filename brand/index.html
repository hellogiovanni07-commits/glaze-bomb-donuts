Offer rules

One signature offer. One loyalty card. Nothing else.

Fewer offers are easier to remember and protect the price of a donut. Seasonal flavours are news, not discounts. Combos are menu bundles, not offers: each one must cost less than buying its items separately.

Do

  • Name the flavour and the filling
  • Say exact times ("fresh at 5 AM")
  • Write like you talk
  • One idea per line
  • Use Caveat for asides of four words or fewer

Don't

  • Call it a "donut hole" or "holeless"
  • Say "guilty pleasure", "guilt" or "cheat day"
  • Write whole sentences in Caveat
  • Stack three exclamation marks
  • Promise health benefits

Logo

A filled donut seen from the side, glaze dripping over the edge. No hole, on purpose. It pairs with a two-tone wordmark in Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold.

Three ways to write the name and only these

The logo

Icon + two-tone wordmark, no space between the words. Signs, boxes, cups, posts, merch.

The stamp

All caps, wide spacing, one colour. Only for box bands, tape and small labels, never next to the logo.

Visit Glaze Bomb on Glaze Lane.

In a sentence

Normal text, two words, in the font around it. Never two-tone, never bold just because it's the name.

The family pick by space

Icon colour versions

Clear space keep it breathing

Leave at least the height of the "x" (half the icon) empty on every side. Nothing else lives inside the dashed line.

Minimum size

Smallest: 20 mm in print, 80 px on screen
Icon 16 px

Below these sizes, use the icon on its own.

Photos shouldn't

Motion

Things fall, bounce and settle, like a donut dropped into the box. Never spin or squash the donut. Demos play only when you press Play.

The drop

Intro and add-to-box. Falls in, bounces twice, settles. 1.2 s, bounce-out.

Toolkit & checklist

Download the files, then go through every material. "Design ready" means the design is final in this kit; a printer still makes the print file.

Logo and artwork files (SVG)

Text in the files uses Plus Jakarta Sans and Caveat. Install the fonts, or convert text to outlines before printing.

Design tokens & details

Before you print

  • PDF, CMYK, 300 dpi, 3 mm bleed
  • Glaze Orange as Pantone 1505 C spot on boxes; ask for a printed proof
  • Text at least 3 mm inside the trim
  • Replace every placeholder first (use "Highlight placeholders")
  • Have a person confirm the allergen card against the real recipes
