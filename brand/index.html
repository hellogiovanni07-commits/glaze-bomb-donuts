Do

  • Name the flavour and the filling
  • Say exact times ("fresh at 5 AM")
  • Write like you talk
  • Keep it to one idea per line
  • Use Caveat for the little asides

Don't

  • Call it a "donut hole" or "holeless"
  • Say "guilty pleasure" or "cheat day"
  • Use ALL CAPS sentences
  • Stack three exclamation marks
  • Promise health benefits

Photos should

  • Show the filling, always
  • Use warm, natural light
  • Leave room for a headline
  • Have real crumbs and real hands

Photos shouldn't

  • Use cold blue light or heavy filters
  • Show a donut with a hole (not ours)
  • Use stock photos of other shops' donuts
  • Crop the drip off the edge

Motion

Things fall, bounce and settle, like a donut dropped into the box. Never spin or squash the donut.

The drop

Intro and add-to-box. Falls in, bounces twice, settles. 1.2 s, bounce-out.

Sprinkle burst

When a box is complete. 28 sprinkles fly out and fade. 0.9 s.

+ Add to box
Button pop

Press feedback: shrink to 92%, overshoot to 106%, settle. 0.5 s.
