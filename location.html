12 Glaze Lane, Downtown.

Checking today's hours…

On the corner of Glaze Lane and Baker Street, a two-minute walk from Downtown station. Look for the orange awning and the neon donut in the window.

Opening hours

    We bake from 5 AM and often sell out early, so mornings have the full range.

    Getting here

    By train
    Downtown station, exit B. Walk down Main Avenue, then left onto Glaze Lane (2 min).
    By car
    20 free spots in the Baker Street lot, 1 minute away. Free for 30 minutes.
    By bike
    Bike rack right by our front door.
    Delivery
    Choose delivery in your order and pick a time.

    Picking up an order

    1. Order ahead on the menu page and pick a time.
    2. Come to the orange shelf just inside the door.
    3. Find your name on the box and the free drink next to it.

    Good to know

    Can I sit in?

    Yes: 12 window seats and a long counter. Come early on weekends.

    Is it wheelchair accessible?

    Yes. Step-free entrance and an accessible restroom.

    Can I bring my dog?

    Dogs are welcome on the terrace, and there is always a water bowl out.

    How do I pay?

    Card, phone or cash at the counter. Online orders are paid when you pick them up.
